Effective handover — best practice

Trigger2 working days before each person's first day off.
  • ✓Start the handover document and complete handover notes; book a call with your buddy.
  • ✓Introduce the buddy and make them fully aware of where every role stands — which need applications reviewed, and which need sourcing.
  • ✓Confirm likely capacity and bandwidth; decide if a third recruiter is needed to cover certain roles or workloads.
  • ✓Send over role kick-off documents so cover fully understands requirements and can source effectively.
  • ✓Make clear the likely offers and the admin that may be needed, based on how far along each role's hiring activity is.
  • ✓Intro end-stage candidates via email with the buddy and/or a third talent partner when needed.
  • ✓Ensure your PTO is clearly marked on your calendar, with the buddy to be contacted named in the subject. Copy the EMEA Team into the calendar event.
